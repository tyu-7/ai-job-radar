# Scoring rubric

Use this rubric only after a role passes the hard eligibility checks. Score each dimension with evidence from the job description and candidate profile; unknown fields receive neither optimistic assumptions nor invented points.

| Dimension | Points | What to assess |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Role and work-content fit | 25 | Actual responsibilities match preferred role families; penalize misleading titles |
| Evidence-backed capability fit | 20 | Candidate has defensible experience for the JD's main requirements |
| Company and business fit | 15 | Preferred industry, business maturity, platform quality, and product exposure |
| Growth opportunity | 15 | Ownership, mentorship, learning density, internal mobility, and strategic relevance |
| Location fit | 10 | Ranked location preference and relocation feasibility |
| Recruiting urgency | 10 | Freshness, deadline proximity, and benefit of applying now |
| Risk fit | 5 | Work intensity, role ambiguity, company stage, travel, or other stated concerns |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Priority bands

- `P0 — Apply now`: 88–100 and no unresolved hard-risk item.
- `P1 — Prioritize`: 80–87.
- `P2 — Selective`: 70–79.
- `P3 — Watchlist`: below 70 or material facts remain unverified.

Move a role to the watchlist regardless of score when eligibility, job status, or the direct source cannot be verified. Do not present the score as an admission probability.
